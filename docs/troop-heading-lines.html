Island Battles · Issue #89 · Design study

Where is each troop group heading?

Every moving group gets a solid, quiet line to its destination castle with an arrowhead on the castle's circle. The board has to stay clean where lines cross, converge, overlap or run under things. Each case below draws synthetic data in the real battlefield's SVG style (same field colour, castle radius, group size, labels and clash overlay) at the scale the lobby shows it. Pick a letter per case, e.g. “1B, 2C, …”. Lines are always opaque tints of the team colour, never transparent, so overlaps cannot make a darker blot.

All data is invented. Lines run from a group's centre (hidden under the group) to the castle's circle; the real renderer would take the position and destination_castle_id from each frame's groups.
